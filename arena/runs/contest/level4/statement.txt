YOUR SCORE IS YOUR TOURNAMENT RATING
Every game is rated with Glicko-2, an Elo-style rating. Everyone starts at 1500.
Beating a stronger team gains more than beating a weaker one; losses cost points. Your score
goes up and down.
Early games move your rating a lot, later games much less.
Your score appears on the contest leaderboard once your current model has played 8 games and
is updated after every game after that.
Uploading a new model keeps your rating but makes it swing more for a while and restarts the
8-game count. Upload when the new model is clearly better.
Ranking: highest level first, then highest score. The score at the end of the contest counts.
Upload your model early. Models submitted near the end of the contest play fewer games, so
their final ranking may be less accurate.

NEW PIECE “TRACE”
Move: 1 tile orthogonally onto an empty tile.
Shoot: the first piece on a diagonal within 3 tiles is the target; if it is an enemy at
distance 2 or 3 it is removed (the Trace does not move).
– An enemy at distance 1 cannot be shot.
NEW PIECE “DAEMON”
Move: Up to 3 tiles orthogonally. Cannot pass through any piece.
– At range 1 an enemy blocks; it cannot be captured this way.
– At range 2 or 3 an enemy is captured by moving onto it.
Shoot: Remove any adjacent enemy piece (any direction). The Daemon does not move.
NEW PIECE “ICE”
Move: Up to 2 tiles orthogonally or diagonally. Path must be clear.
Capture: By moving onto an enemy piece.
Guards: the first of Ping, Exploit, Trace, Daemon, Ice next to the Kernel is hit.

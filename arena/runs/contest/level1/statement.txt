BOARD
• 8×8 tiles. Two teams: CYAN (you) and MAGENTA. On the
board, K = Kernel and P = Ping.
• Turn-based: a turn is exactly two actions by one team.
CYAN starts.
• An action is one piece moving or shooting. The same piece
may act twice. You cannot skip an action.
• A round is CYAN's turn followed by MAGENTA's turn.
KERNEL
• Per action, the Kernel moves 1 step to any adjacent tile.
An enemy piece on that tile is captured.
• The Kernel is the centerpiece of the game: lose it and you
lose immediately; capture the enemy Kernel and you win.
PING
• Move: up to 2 tiles orthogonally, onto empty tiles only.
It cannot jump over pieces.
• Capture: the first piece on a diagonal within 2 tiles, if
it is an enemy: the Ping moves onto it.
• The Ping cannot capture orthogonally and cannot move
diagonally onto an empty tile.

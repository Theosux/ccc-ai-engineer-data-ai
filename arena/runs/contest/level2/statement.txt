KERNEL PROTECTION (BODYGUARD)
A Kernel that has a friendly piece in any of the 8 adjacent tiles is
protected (marked by a shield icon). When a protected Kernel is attacked (by
move or shot), the protecting piece is destroyed instead:
• The Kernel survives. The attacker stays on its tile, even if the attack
was a move.
• Each attack removes one guard only: a second attack can hit the Kernel.
The firewall ignores guards.
FIREWALL
• The board shrinks: at the end of rounds 8, 16, 24 and 26 (after MAGENTA's
turn) its outermost ring of tiles disappears for good: 8×8 → 6×6 → 4×4 →
2×2 → none.
• During those rounds the ring about to disappear is striped yellow. Pieces
on it when it disappears are destroyed.
• If the firewall destroys a Kernel, the other team wins. If it destroys
both Kernels at once, MAGENTA wins.
• Therefore, a game lasts at most 26 rounds: if nobody has won by then,
MAGENTA wins. There are no draws.
KERNEL ENDGAME
If only the two Kernels are left at the end of a turn…
• Within 2 tiles of each other (diagonals count): the team that just
finished its turn loses;
• Otherwise, the Kernel on the more central ring wins (central 2×2, then
4×4, then 6×6, then the edge); same ring: MAGENTA wins.

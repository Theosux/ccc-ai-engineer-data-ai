# Rules and AI policy (Data/AI)

## Official rules (all tracks)
Source: https://codingcontest.org/info/how-it-works, https://codingcontest.org/terms/overview
- "On-site participants are not allowed to perform excessive downloads or stream content during the contest."
  → **pre-download** wheels and model weights before the contest.
- "Teams may use multiple laptops, and every team member can submit solutions on behalf of the team."
- One account per person; minimum age 16 (14 in Austria); venue-specific rules emailed before the event prevail.
- Any programming language ("Any language, any tool", game client).

## AI / LLM policy
- **No public rule restricts AI, LLMs, pretrained models, external data or GPUs**, for any track. The verifier
  searched all 136 platform JS chunks, the Terms and the Birds client (verification report, 2026-10-19).
- The track is marketed as "if you are into training your own AI — pick the #data/ai track!"
  (https://codeforces.com/blog/entry/147433) and "Data/AI: Let your model find the perfect algorithms"
  (https://www.cloudflight.io/en/event/42-cloudflight-coding-contest/).
- Teo cleared scripted download/submit with the organizers for Classic (2026-10-09); the Data/AI game uses the same
  client and API.
